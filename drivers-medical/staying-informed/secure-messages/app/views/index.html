Drivers Medical prototype

Secure messages: all screens

Today, anything with medical content goes to Drivers Medical customers by second-class post. Secure messages is an authenticated, two-way channel where customers read those letters and act on them, bound to their case record.

GOV.UK Notify is the doorbell. Secure messages is the room behind the door.

{{ designNote("Four rules every screen respects. Notify pings, secure messages holds the content. Sign-in is the gate for reading, downloading and replying. Every interaction is an event on the case. Channel choice is the customer’s, but the record is single.") }}

Open questions

Each one also appears on the screen it affects, when design notes are on.

  • Legal (Keya): which letters serve a notice, what counts as served, and does a secure copy alongside paper raise any issue?
  • IAG: the assurance floor for the paper code (single-use or reusable, expiry, what happens if it is lost), and retention if messages never expire.
  • Content: the rule for what is safe for Notify and what must go through secure messages.
  • Which letters first: waiting on volumes per template. Driver questionnaires are the leading candidate.
  • Where it lives: the Dynamics customer account, or GOV.UK App Mailbox.
