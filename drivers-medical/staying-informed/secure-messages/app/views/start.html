You have a secure message from DVLA

We use secure messages for letters about your health and your driving licence, so that only you can read them.

You need to sign in to read your message, download a copy or reply. This is how we know it is you.

We have also posted you a paper copy.

Is this message genuine?

Our texts and emails about secure messages:

  • never say what the message is about
  • never ask for bank details or payment
  • always link to a page on www.gov.uk

If you are not sure a text or email is from us, do not use the link in it. Go to GOV.UK and search for “DVLA secure messages” instead.
