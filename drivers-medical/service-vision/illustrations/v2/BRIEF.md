# Illustration brief: whole-service map v2

The step illustrations for `../../whole-service-map-v2.html`. One image per step, named by map and step number in the order the steps appear (`cx-01.webp` to `cx-25.webp`, `newco-01.webp` to `newco-18.webp`). The images already in this folder are the style reference: match them.

## Rules

- Soft flat illustration, light blue rooms and skies, rounded shapes, gentle texture. Match the existing images.
- Portrait, 4:5.
- No text, numbers, logos or crowns anywhere. Never show a GOV.UK page, a DVLA sign or a real brand. Screens, letters and cards show simple shapes only.
- **CX map:** the customer is always the same man: messy brown hair, green jumper, navy trousers. Vary where he is (kitchen, garden, bus, street, waiting room). He fits this around his life rather than sitting at a desk.
- **NewCo map:** the agent is a woman with a ponytail, a headset and a blue top. Other staff and customers can vary, but nobody on the NewCo map wears the CX customer's green jumper.
- Every step is one moment. Don't show the outcome of a decision unless the step is about that outcome.

### CX map (customer)

- **cx-03** Sign in or create an account: on the sofa, signing in on his phone, a little impatient.
- **cx-12** Consent to share your data: at the kitchen table, reading a short consent screen on his phone, considering it.
- **cx-18** Understand the decision: in his garden, reading a letter carefully, thoughtful.
- **cx-20** Challenge the decision: at the kitchen counter writing a short letter, a medical report beside him, determined.
- **cx-22** Get a renewal reminder: on the bus, glancing at a reminder notification on his phone.
- **cx-23** Renew: leaning on the kitchen counter completing a short form on his phone, relaxed.
- **cx-24** Report a change: in a GP waiting room, updating his details on his phone.
- **cx-25** Make a complaint: out walking, on the phone, frowning as he explains a problem.

### NewCo map (operations)

- **newco-05** Customer notifies or renews: a screen where cards arrive from a phone, a laptop and an envelope into one queue.
- **newco-16** Proactive: outbound: the agent at her computer sending updates that fly out to several phones.
