Drive with a medical condition: the journey we're working towards

One person's route through the target-state service, from wondering whether to tell DVLA to living with the decision. It is the customer row of the whole-service map, cut down to what someone would notice, with today beside it for contrast.

You always know whether you can drive, what happens next and roughly when, without having to ask.

How it should feel in the target state How it feels today Stage scope not yet agreed Open question The feeling curve is a hypothesis for discussion, not research.

It is not a straight line

One case, three things at once

Stay informed, provide what's needed and get help are not steps in order. They run side by side for as long as the case is open, and the quality of the first decides how much of the third happens.

A decision is not the end

Most people on a medical licence come back: to renew, to tell us something has changed, or to challenge. Each return should start from what we already know, not from a blank form.

Bad news still needs a clear route

Refused or revoked is the lowest point on the curve whatever we do. The target is that it is clear, reasoned and comes with what to do next, not that it feels good.
