Drivers Medical: target state, NewCo operations

Working draft. High-level view of how the future operation runs. Who makes the decision is still to be agreed.

Across every stage
Decision quality must not fall. Every hypothesis could be met by a faster service making worse licensing decisions.
We want to improve CSAT, quality, and the effectiveness and happiness of staff.
Hypotheses the tasks test
Universal role: casework and customer contact become one job.
EOC support model: EOC supports in real time rather than as an escalation route.
Ownership of a customer group: one team owns a group of customers, not just a case.
Measures built around customers: KPIs shift from individual throughput to team customer outcomes.
Proactive engagement: agents contact customers before they chase, and use every call.
Communications and guidance: clearer guidance, transparency and reasons build trust.
