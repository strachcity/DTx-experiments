Where technology and service changes sit on the Drivers Medical journey

The top row shows the stages of the target service, from the whole-service map. Under them is the current-state journey. Greyed columns are target stages with nothing in today's journey, left blank unless something acts there. Below it are two bands, each ranked by impact on the service: seven technology enablers in colour, and seven service changes (policy, operations and commercial) in black. Select any item to see what it depends on, or a stage to see what acts on it.

One keystoneThe condition and the customer's answers held as data at intake. Most of the other enablers rely on it.
Two different prizesMessages and the case timeline reduce chasing calls. HCP evidence reduces decision time. They should be argued separately.
The backlog is complex casesSpeeding up simple flow won't shrink the backlog much on its own. That depends on stages 6 and 7.
Mostly H2- technologyFour of the seven technology enablers, as scoped, sustain today's service. Most of the transforming work is service change and data.
Do now, unlock laterCommissioning evidence and asking only what decides are within reach now. Checking decisions is what lets policy agree to the rest.

H1

Current system. Today's Drivers Medical: the journey lanes on the map. It works well enough to persist but fails people in critical ways.

H2+

Transforming. Changes the conditions the service runs under: rules, contracts, review cycles, measures, data. Slow to pay off and hard to attribute.

H2-

Sustaining. Real short-term value that relieves the pressure for structural change. Fine when it's deliberate and named honestly.

H3

Future system. Working assumption: proportionate decisions, one declaration, evidence only where it changes the outcome, review on change, decisions checked against outcomes.

Labels follow the three horizons framework in Eating Policy. The test for each item: does it move the service toward H3, or prolong H1? Classification depends on the H3 vision, which is a draft here.
