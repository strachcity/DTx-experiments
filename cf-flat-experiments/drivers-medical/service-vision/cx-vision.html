DM CX vision

People who tell DVLA about a medical condition want to know two things: can they keep driving, and what happens next. Too often today they repeat themselves, wait without hearing anything, and end up chasing. More than half the calls we get are people asking where their case is.

We're building a service that tells people what's happening before they need to ask. It treats each person in proportion to their risk, and it reaches a safe decision as quickly as the case allows.

Six shifts people will feel

1

Tell us once

You tell us about your condition once, in the way that suits you: online, by phone or on paper. We keep what you've told us, so you never start from scratch next time.

Today

You declare on a D1, then answer much of it again on a questionnaire. ~33,000 questionnaires a year are rejected for missing information.

How we'll know

  • Complete first time
  • Paper keyed by hand

What makes it possible

  • 1Structured data at intake
  • 11Storing data against the customer record
  • S3Ask only what decides the case
2

A decision in a day, or a clear next step

Where we can decide straight away, we do it within a day of having what we need. Where we can't, you always know what happens next, roughly when, and whether you can keep driving in the meantime.

Today

A caseworker reads each paper notification and picks the questionnaire by hand. Simple cases wait in the same queue as complex ones, though 86.5% of online cases need no escalation or evidence (DMSP cases only).

How we'll know

  • Routed right first time
  • Average days to decision

What makes it possible

  • 2Rules and workflow layer in Dynamics
  • S6Run separate routes for different kinds of case

Not yet covered: nothing on the capability map answers "can I keep driving in the meantime?"

3

Never wonder where your case is

We tell you when your case moves, before you need to call. You can see your case and your letters online, and anyone you speak to knows where it's up to.

Today

~56% of contacts are people chasing progress, costing about £1.8m a year. Around 296,000 letters a year only say "we've written to your doctor".

How we'll know

  • Progress-chasing calls
  • Repeat calls

What makes it possible

  • 3Correspondence events and automated Notify
  • 10Secure communication and case updates
  • 7Contact routing, so agents can say how long
4

Evidence without the wait

We only ask for medical evidence when it will change the decision. We ask healthcare professionals only what you can't tell us yourself, we get it quickly, and you can send us what you already have.

Today

50,000 to 55,000 letters a month go to healthcare professionals, and they take a median of ~30 days to come back.

How we'll know

  • Evidence requests per case
  • Days waiting on third parties

What makes it possible

  • 5HCP evidence exchange
  • S2Commission evidence instead of requesting it
  • 4Secure digital return channel
5

A licence that fits your condition

If your condition is stable, your licence reflects that. We check in lightly, and we look again when your health changes.

Today

Short licences bring the same people back within three years, and each renewal re-runs the same casework.

How we'll know

  • Effort per renewal
  • Renewals without a gap
  • Changes reported before review

What makes it possible

  • 6Flexible licence durations in D90
  • S4Review on change, not on the date

Already happening: 17 chronic neurological conditions have renewed on declaration since October 2021.

6

Decisions you can trust, and can challenge

Decisions are made against clear standards and come with reasons. If you disagree, there's a fair and simple way to ask us to look again.

Today

Heart, neurological and cognitive cases go to doctors without a written standard to decide against, and nothing routinely checks decisions afterwards.

How we'll know

  • Decision quality
  • Challenges and appeals
  • Appeals won

What makes it possible

  • S1Explicit thresholds for judgement-heavy conditions
  • 9Single source of truth for operating instructions and policy
  • S5Check how declaration-based decisions hold up

Not yet covered: nothing on the capability map covers the route to challenge a decision.

What changes for our teams

  • Cases arrive ready to work.
  • Each team owns a group of customers.
  • Agents contact customers before they chase.
  • Clinical support is there in the moment, so agents don't have to escalate to get it.
  • We're measured on outcomes for customers as well as throughput.

What stays the same

  • Paper stays for anyone who wants it.
  • Safety comes first. Decision quality must not fall.
